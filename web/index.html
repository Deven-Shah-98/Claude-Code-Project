    How this works

    Question. After a venue opened, did nearby home values grow faster or slower than they otherwise would have?

    Method. For each venue, ZIP codes within 3 miles are the treated area. A synthetic twin is a weighted blend of ZIP codes from elsewhere in the country (none within 15 miles, matched on land area) chosen to track the treated area's home-value path for the 5 years before opening. The orange line is that twin; the gap after opening is the estimated effect. On the map, each column's color shows how much faster (blue) or slower (red) that ZIP grew than the twin would have.

    Is it real? The same procedure is run on random areas that have no venue. The placebo p-value is how often one of those looks as extreme as the venue. With 40 placebos the smallest possible p is about 0.024.

    Limits. This is an association, not proof of causation: stadiums are often part of larger redevelopments. Venues opening in 2019–20 are labelled pandemic-confounded. Venues from 2000–02 cannot be scored because the data starts in 2000. Columns show ZIP centroids, not boundaries.

    Data. Zillow Home Value Index (ZHVI) by ZIP · US Census ZCTA gazetteer · Venue locations verified against Wikidata. All public.
